# R8660: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio, 1710

- **DECODE:** record R8660, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8660
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key: this letter has no clerk's decipherment on the page.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

An appeal for protection and expressions of gratitude are followed by distrust of the government. A swordfight, subsequent confinement, and a dispute involving contraband grain are described.
