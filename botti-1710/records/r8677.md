# R8677: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio

- **DECODE:** record R8677, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8677
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext. This letter carries a clerk's decipherment on the page; `../README.md` gives the agreement on the 29 letters of that kind.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

Monsignore Marabottini finally returns from Ferrara after waiting patiently for an audience, while Abbot Bossetti went back and forth many times without settling anything and was not received. Later pages describe further journeys, doubt over the flattering account of the reception, unreported Carnival abuses, and close connections among officials.
