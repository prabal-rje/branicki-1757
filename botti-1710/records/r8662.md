# R8662: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio, 1710

- **DECODE:** record R8662, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8662
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key: this letter has no clerk's decipherment on the page.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

Permission to leave is being withheld pending another party's satisfaction. Limited leave is obtained, and the writer plans to travel away and await the outcome of the persecution.

## The rows as read

`·` is a code that the read leaves open. `?` is a stretch that is not read or is not a code. A space is a space of the page. The number at the start of a line is the row of the transcription.
