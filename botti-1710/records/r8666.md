# R8666: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio, 1710

- **DECODE:** record R8666, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8666
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key: this letter has no clerk's decipherment on the page. Page T005 is too small.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

The writer notes that with the last ordinary post his persecution under the government of Cardinal Ruffo ended; he mentions Marabottini, Ruffo in Imola, and an effort to engage someone against him.
