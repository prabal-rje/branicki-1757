# R8470: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio, 1710

- **DECODE:** record R8470, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8470
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext, with the key of the letters with dots. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

Botti writes letter after letter, then goes in person. The Abbate refuses to see him and makes a noise about it. There is talk of prison. Farsetti went to Cesena on the Abbate's order. A man complains that he was betrayed and deceived, and asks to be set free. Botti blames "the malice of the lieutenant".
