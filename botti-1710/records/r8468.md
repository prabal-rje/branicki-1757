- **DECODE:** record R8468, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8468
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext, with the key of the letters with dots. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

Botti had fever and a bad headache, so he could not write, and he asks pardon. Ferrara is ill. Ruffo said that he would give Botti time until he was well. Then he ordered the lieutenant to serve Botti another injunction ("precetto"). Botti says that he is wronged, and he thinks of leaving. He names the Abbate, a count, and a return from Florence.
