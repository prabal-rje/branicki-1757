# R8688: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio, 1711

- **DECODE:** record R8688, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8688
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext. This letter carries a clerk's decipherment on the page; `../README.md` gives the agreement on the 29 letters of that kind.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

A mission has not secured restitution of the tower. Occupying troops retain property and demand maintenance, and the writer suspects understandings between the courts.

## The rows as read

`·` is a code that the read leaves open. `?` is a stretch that is not read or is not a code. A space is a space of the page. The number at the start of a line is the row of the transcription.
