# R8618: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio, 1710

- **DECODE:** record R8618, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8618
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext, with the key of the letters with dots. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

The dispute over precedence goes on: the chapters of the treasury do not touch this point.

## The rows as read

`·` is a letter that the reading leaves open. Capital words are word codes of the key. A space is a space of the page. The number at the start of a line is the row of the transcription.
