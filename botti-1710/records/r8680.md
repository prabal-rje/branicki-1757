# R8680: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio, 1711

- **DECODE:** record R8680, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8680
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext. This letter carries a clerk's decipherment on the page; `../README.md` gives the agreement on the 29 letters of that kind.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

The writer alleges cash bargaining over pardons and release, pressure on people accused of crimes, and harsher confinement when a larger payment is refused. Later passages describe conspicuous social favoritism.
