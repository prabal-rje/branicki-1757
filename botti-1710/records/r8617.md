# R8617: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio, 1710

- **DECODE:** record R8617, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8617
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext, with the key of the letters with dots. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

A quarrel at Bertinoro involves don Antonio, nephew of Ruffo. Monsignor Farsetti has neither the favour nor the esteem of Ruffo. After the midday meal, Ruffo goes away to write. Don Antonio comes out of Ruffo's room in good humour, claps his hands, and gives Botti's signed memorial to the delegate.
