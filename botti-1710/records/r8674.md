# R8674: Botti to Cardinal Gualterio

- **DECODE:** record R8674, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/8674
- **Archive:** British Library, Add MS 20443 (papers of Cardinal F. A. Gualterio).
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key: this letter has no clerk's decipherment on the page.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.
- **Key values from the clerk:** 14 word codes of the key take their value from a clerk's decipherment on the page, not from my key. `../key.tsv` marks each one.

## English gloss

Page T001 mentions a comedy of actors ("comedia d'istrioni"), a restless nature, and Monsignore Marabottini repenting of having given license. Later broken passages concern intrigue among church and government officials, favorable letters to the central court, and exemption from financial impositions.
