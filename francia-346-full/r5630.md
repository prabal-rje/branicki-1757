## The leaves

- f. 174r: a short letter in clear (the transcription reads its year as 1683). Rome has the originals and copies of Ceva's despatches of 13, 20 and 22 December.
- f. 175r: the cipher letter of 5 February 1633, to the extraordinary nuncio in France.

## Summary

Each point ends with its source: the decode, the decipherment of the time between the rows, or the clear text. All three are machine transcriptions. The words are mine.

- Rome hears that the French court has agreed to send plenipotentiaries to a third place. (decode for the plenipotentiaries; decipherment of the time for the place)
- One thing could cut off the hopes: that others, impatient with delays, might rush to help the Protestants. (decipherment of the time; the decode leaves codes 538, 918 and 921 open)
- While there is hope on all sides, Ceva must urge a resolution in [347] and in Germany. (decode; code 347 open)
