## The leaves

- f. 183v: a docket in clear (Cardinal Barberini; the business of the Bishop of Chalcedon).
- f. 184r: a letter in clear of 12 February, signed by Francesco Barberini. It praises Ceva's letter of 4 January.
- f. 185r: the cipher letter of 19 February 1633, to the extraordinary nuncio in France.

## Summary

Each point ends with its source: the decode, the decipherment of the time between the rows, or the clear text. All three are machine transcriptions. The words are mine.

- Ceva proposed a way to settle a matter about the day of a cavalcade. Rome will weigh it. (decode; the decipherment of the time names the Duke of Créqui)
- The French [?] asked, through Monsieur Gueffier, that some monasteries be given suitable members. (decode; code 831 a conjecture)
- Gueffier excused some religious who swore an oath without his knowledge. (decode)
