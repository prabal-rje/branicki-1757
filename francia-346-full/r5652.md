- **Heading:** my transcription notes give no heading for the cipher pages. Other pages of the record end "Roma 3. Sett." and "Roma x Set[t?].ʳᵉ". The machine transcription reads their year as 1623 and 1643.
- **Clear text before f. 303r C01:** it acknowledges a letter of the nuncio "de 16. del passato" with a copy of a writing given by Benavides.

## Summary

Rome comments on the nuncio's talk with a person (code 797) and with Benavides. It says that two fortresses left with a party (code 831) would become almost impossible to restore. To take them back by force is full of difficulty and danger. f. 303v reads in part: it names talks in Vienna and Benavides.
