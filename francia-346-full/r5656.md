## Summary

The published key reads this page only in fragments. The page has the mark of the 8-to-6 misreading: a high share of 6 and a low share of 8. A declared variant, in which a 6 may stand for a misread 8, reads "caduta di Ratisbola" (perhaps Ratisbona, Regensburg) and "perdita veramente considerabile" in C04 and C05. This page reports that variant only as a hint.
