## Summary

A companion letter to [R5638](r5638.md) on the same priory and pension settlement. The writer notes that he had requested something ("aveva richiesto") and speaks of affection and prudence ("affetto", "prudenza") and not acting out of self-interest ("non per interesse di veruna sorte"). He lays out an arrangement combining a pension ("con penzione"), another compensation ("nell'altra ricompenza"), adding a payment of four [thousand] ("aggiungere il dar di quattro"), and taking the equivalent and remainder of the priory ("e l'equivalente del priorato ... pigliare il rimanente"), together with other conveniences for his satisfaction ("per mia sodisfazione") and a reply on the news of his arrival ("in risposta l'avviso del suo arrivo").
