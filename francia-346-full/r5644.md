## The leaves

- f. 257r: a letter in clear of 4 June 1633, signed Barberini: no despatch came this week.

## Summary

Each point ends with its source: the decode, the decipherment of the time between the rows, or the clear text. All three are machine transcriptions. The words are mine.

- Ceva was prudent not to go to a place where a promotion of cardinals was to be made. (clear text)
- A writing of the proposals of Benavides was handed on. (decode)
- The person who handed it on is Father Joseph. (decipherment of the time; the decode has two open codes inside the name)
- Rome charges Ceva to use this opening to bring the business to its end. (clear text)
