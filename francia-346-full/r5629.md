## The leaves

- f. 171v: a docket in clear that names Cardinal Barberini.
- f. 172r: a letter in clear of 22 January, signed by Cardinal Barberini. It asks Ceva to report on the business of the Bishop of Chalcedon.
- f. 173r: the cipher letter of 23 January. A clear opening says that the courier for Brussels carries it.
