## Summary

Rome judges the talk between [581] and Benavides useful: it prepares S.E. not to reject at once the answer to the writing. The nuncio must work for an answer that keeps the talks going. Rome fears that the [831] want to finish everything at once, and it sees the affairs of Germany as full of difficulty. On f. 288v, Rome prefers to begin with one separate business, as an introduction to the universal [?peace].
