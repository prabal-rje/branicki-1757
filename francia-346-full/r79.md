## Summary

From the decode with the known key. The words are mine.

- Rome, after 28 August 1632. No news of Madrid's mind on the union and on the restitution of a fortress; the Swedes are more dangerous to France than to anyone; the Huguenots; the heretics in Germany and Flanders.
