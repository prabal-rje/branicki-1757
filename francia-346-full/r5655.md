## Summary

Letters from Germany (codes 930 957) report on "Monsieur della Carboniera" and on a move of arms. A neutrality is said to be violated, which a party holds with the Swedes and with France; the Imperial court is displeased. Rome sees the hope of a [?peace] between the crowns move away each day, and it speaks of the handling of "this holy work". A meeting in a third place, with a suspension of arms, could serve.
