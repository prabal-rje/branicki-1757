## Summary

Rome hears from several sides that a woman (code 175) is ill and in danger. It wishes that she be reconciled with a "f…uolo" (perhaps a son) and return. Rome has read the writing given to Benavides and foresees that nothing will come of it. On f. 268v, Rome says that wanting everything goes against good maxims, and that someone finds more readiness in S.E. than before.

## Notes

- The decode does not name the woman. Code 175 also stands in R5648 f. 279v C14, beside an illness.
- Code 470 ("hor") does not fit in f. 268r C14 or in f. 268v C13. The reading marks it.
- f. 268r fails the page gate of the key-free check (P5 0.600). Its text still reads in large part.
