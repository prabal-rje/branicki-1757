## Summary

Rome answers letters of the first of the previous month and of 26 February, on a good [?peace] and a priory of San Ma[…]ino. It reports a rumour: a party (code 831), for a reward, wants a fortress on this side of the Rhine that the Swedes hold. It gives the answer of "S.E." on the plenipotentiaries and on the fortresses of Lorraine. The nuncio must keep the confidence of a person (codes 330 577).
