# R80: Rome to the nuncio, 1632

DECODE's own transcription of the record, decoded with the published key as DECODE gives it (see README.md) and with some values from decipherments of the time.

- **DECODE:** record R80 ("ASV_i1025_SdS_France_346-7"), https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/80. Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346.
- **Credits:** DECODE record R80 (DECRYPT project) and DECODE's transcription of it; Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346; the key: the published key, as DECODE gives it (see README.md).
- **State:** from key (the key that George Lasry published on DECODE, as DECODE gives it, with 28 three-digit values taken from decipherments of the time and from context, and 2 values corrected from a decipherment of the time). README.md lists each of the 30 values with its source.
- **Read before:** others read these leaves before: the cipher ledger's notes say that George Lasry read R79 to R82, and the ledger itself read R84 in October 2026.
- **A decipherment of the time:** a clerk wrote a decipherment above the cipher rows of some leaves of this series. This page does not copy it.

## Summary

From the decode with the known key. The words are mine.

- Rome, 13 ... 1632. Plenipotentiaries in a third place for the union; France will not show a treaty with the Imperials and Spaniards without sure effect; secrecy under oath; a congress.
