## Summary

Rome praises the nuncio's opening with [581] on peace and union, and wants no one to irritate minds with aid and incitement. It warns twice against breaking the thread of the talks, and it names a break into war with [113]. On f. 279v, Rome regrets that the nuncio did not see a writing at once, and it regrets the illness of [175]. The end names a receipt for the payment of a price.
