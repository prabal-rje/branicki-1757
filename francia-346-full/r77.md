## Summary

From the decode with the known key, in a machine transcription. The words are mine.

- Ceva's main business is the union of the princes [?] (code 110, a conjecture). Some think that it only makes France stronger.
- The first aim of S.M. [?] (code 107, a conjecture) was to bring in a general peace.
- A settlement was asked for, on behalf of the King [?] (code 170, a conjecture), in the business of Monsieur.
- Ceva must make known in [347] (open) and in Germany that his mission serves the general peace.
