## Summary

A short, broken letter in the same key and business as [R5638](r5638.md) and [R5639](r5639.md). The readable stretches concern compensation ("ricompenza") for pensions ("queste penzioni"), letting a pension suffer ("far patire la penzione"), opportunities ("nelle occasioni che ha"), and a particular obligation ("tenuto particolarmente"). Many rows are broken by unread stretches in the transcription.
