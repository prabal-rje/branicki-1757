## Summary

Benavides has already given a written answer, which does not seem to satisfy. Rome says that proposals and answers, carried back and forth, settle matters, and that the nuncio must keep the talks going. On f. 297v, Rome discusses aid to the Dutch under old alliances, and it wants the business brought sooner to its end. A claim to a fief and a restitution form a great obstacle.
