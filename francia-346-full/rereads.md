# Francia 346: ten pages read again

My pipeline read ten cipher pages again on 12 and 13 November 2026. These pages read badly, or not at all, in the first transcription. This page decodes the new transcription with the published key, in the same way as the record pages. Every number here is real.

- **The source:** a machine transcription in three model stages (a draft, a check and a reconciliation). Nobody verified it.
- **The record pages keep the first transcription.** Their numbers and the totals of README.md do not include this page.
- **Credits:** DECODE records R5642, R5643, R5656, R5657, R5658 and R5659 (DECRYPT project); Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346; the key: G. Lasry, B. Megyesi and N. Kopal (2020); the ledger values: the cipher ledger, https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver.

## What reads

- **R5643, f. 249r, reads in part.** Benavides says that he will sign, if the French come to reasonable terms. The words of time stay unread. The writer fears that by "reasonable" they mean a restitution.
  - Decode, f. 249r C14 to C18: `che fail {bene}auides chese i[831 ?francesi] uoglionoue nir allecose ragioneuoli eglida un[470 ?hor] adun [734]trusotoscriuera dubito {~pero} che perle coset{~monsieur}gioneuoli intenda[983] resti⟨t⟩⟨u⟩tion·`
  - Open codes: 734 and 983. Codes 831 and 470 are conjectures. Code 938 ("monsieur") inside "ragionevoli" shows a fault of the cut.
- **R5643 f. 249v and R5659 f. 351r** read in fragments only.
- **R5642 f. 246r, R5656, R5657 and R5658** give 5.07 to 5.59 bits per letter. They read in short stretches. I did not read them for content.
