## Summary

The letter speaks of an "ambasciaria" and of men at the court who work for the Church. The nuncio spoke well about the priory and about offers of pensions. Rome regrets that Benavides lacks a power, and it refers to what a party (code 831) did with Botru. The letter ends with an excuse for the delay of the answer.
