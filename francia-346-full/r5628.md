## Summary

Each point ends with its source: the decode, the decipherment of the time between the rows, or the clear text. All three are machine transcriptions. The words are mine.

- Rome says that Ceva overstates the French crown's feeling for Rome. (decipherment of the time)
- Ceva spoke to a person close to the King (code 747, open). He urged the ruin of heresy and the union of the Catholic princes. (decode)
- The settlement of Lorraine looks hard. One side wants to keep [130] and other fortresses; the other wants them returned to those who held them. (decode; code 130 open)
- The grant of the priory of San Martino to Signor Antonio, and the King's favour through Ceva. (decode and decipherment of the time)
- Rome approves what Ceva said: the King must not take new commitments with Germany after the death of the Swede. (decode; codes 420 open and 170 a conjecture)
