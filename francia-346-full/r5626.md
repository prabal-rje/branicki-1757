## Summary

Rome answers letters of 30 November and 3 December, and asks that "Monsieur di Botru" stop in a place (code 951). It wants the crowns brought to a perfect correspondence, and it names the death of a person whose name reads only as "S…co". Rome writes separately to Bichi about Venice and Ferrara, and it blames the conduct of a party (code 330). The last rows read only in part.
