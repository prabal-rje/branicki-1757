## Summary

Each point ends with its source in brackets. "Decipherment of the time" is the clerk's text between the cipher rows. "Clear text" is a text in clear on f. 418r or f. 419r. All are machine transcriptions. The words are mine. `[?word]` marks a doubtful word.

- The opening lines speak of the constant worry of all who want peace, above all of those charged to procure it. Besides his cipher of the 19th of last month, the writer sends this courier, Sebastiano Bachin[i]. (decipherment of the time)
- Madrid hears that the French King prepares aid to trouble Flanders, and sends troops in good number to its borders. So, when the Dutch take the field, Spain must divide its forces. Madrid complains strongly: this could lead to an open break and upset every peace negotiation. (decipherment of the time)
- After long efforts, the writer obtained an order from the King of Spain. Ambassador [?Benavides], at the French court, gets a commission to enter the treaty of peace. The [?Marquis] of Aitona in Flanders gets the same, and the Infanta gets notice. (decipherment of the time)
- This agrees with what the addressee and Mons. Bichi proposed. The writer asks the addressee to open the negotiation and take each party "per mano" (by the hand) to an agreement. He has also informed a prelate in Flanders. (decipherment of the time)
- The later letter on f. 418r answers a remark about the courier. The writer trusts that the addressee has corrected every other reading of its purpose. Its only aim was the one in the cipher: to open the way to a settlement. Bautru could not get the faculty to treat; the ambassador ("brauides", probably [?Benavides]) now has it. (clear text)
- The text from Vienna on f. 419r says that the Imperial ministers want peace with the "heretics" of Germany. The writer says that "we" always condemn it as harmful to religion. From the addressee's talks with Cardinal Richelieu, he hopes for an opening to concord between the Catholic crowns. (clear text)

## Notes

- **The month.** The letter cites the writer's cipher "of the 19th of last month". [R5670](r5670.md) is a cipher from Madrid of 19 February 1633. So I read March. This is my inference.
- The writer of the Madrid letter and of f. 418r is not named on these leaves. Nor is the writer of f. 419r.
- [R5666](r5666.md) tells how Bautru came to Madrid in November 1632 without a faculty to treat.
