# R74: Rome to the nuncio, 1632 to 1634 (catalogue dates)

A machine transcription of the DECODE images, made in my pipeline. Nobody verified it. Decoded with the published key and the ledger's values for some codes (see README.md). Every number here is real.

- **DECODE:** record R74 ("ASV_i1025_SdS_France_346-1"), status "Decrypted". Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346.
- **Credits:** DECODE record R74 (DECRYPT project); Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346; the key: G. Lasry, B. Megyesi and N. Kopal (2020); the ledger values: the cipher ledger, https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver.
- **Cipher pages:** f. 071r (DECODE page 1, 11 rows).
- **Key-free check:** none. This record was in the fit of our key-free key.
- **Coverage (first pass, ledger values not graded):** 107 code tokens; 92 with a value (86.0%). Three-digit tokens: 5 with a ledger value, 15 with no value.
- **Three-digit tokens by grade:** certain 2, probable 2, conjecture 6, open 10.
- **Doubt:** 6 units hold a doubtful digit; 0 hold an unread sign.
- **Bits per letter** under an Italian letter model: 4.34. The same letters in a shuffled order give 9.69.
- **A decipherment of the time:** DECODE's catalogue marks "Inline Plaintext: Yes", and the pilot transcription shows text rows between the cipher rows. This page does not copy them.
