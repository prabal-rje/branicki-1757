## Summary

The writer sends an enclosed letter ("l'agiunta lettera") and a separate cipher sheet ("un foglio di cifra") to "V.E." (Your Excellency), leaving it to Your Excellency whether to present or withhold the enclosed letter. The main business is a priory ("il priorato"): if the party desires it, the writer considers renouncing it ("il renuntiarglielo"), noting that giving up the share of the priory would hasten and open the way for him to declare himself ("aprirmi la strada da publicarmi"). He instructs the recipient to take four thousand scudi ("pigliare li quattro mila scudi"), with provision to reimburse himself ("in provizione de refarsi da me"), rather than act as a salaried servant ("servitore provizionato"). He writes in great confidence ("in tanta confidenza") and asks that this matter be kept quiet and not used in any other way.
