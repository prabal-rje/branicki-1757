A machine transcription of the DECODE images, made in my pipeline. Nobody verified it. Decoded with the published key and the ledger's values for some codes (see README.md). Every number here is real.

- **DECODE:** record R5658 ("ASV_i1025_SdS_Francia_346_34"), status "N/A". Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346.
- **Cipher pages:** f. 344r (DECODE page 1, 23 rows); f. 344v (DECODE page 2, 22 rows); f. 345r (DECODE page 3, 5 rows).
- **Key-free check, before the key was opened:** the record too small to judge (no page passes the gate with read digits).
- **Coverage:** 107 code tokens; 97 with a value (90.7%). Three-digit tokens: 3 with a ledger value, 5 with no value. Three-digit codes: 3 with a value, 5 without.
- **Doubt:** 6 units hold a doubtful digit; 5 hold an unread sign.
- **Bits per letter** under an Italian letter model: 4.76. The same letters in a shuffled order give 9.35.

- **Heading:** my transcription notes give no heading for these pages. The clear prose after f. 345r C05 answers the nuncio's letter "de 14 del pas[sato]" on his talk with the King about "l'unione".

## Summary

The source reads no sign in the cipher rows of f. 344r and f. 344v. The five rows of f. 345r read in part. They speak of someone's will, which will always be the same, and of S.E.
