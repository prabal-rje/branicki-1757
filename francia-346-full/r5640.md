## Summary

Rome wants a "ministerio" from both sides, so that the start of a negotiation is not cut off. Talks settle matters, but some things irritate minds and lead to a rupture; the point of [130] will be the most difficult. On the universal [?peace] Rome sees endless difficulties: if two [170] were settled, the others would be easy. Rome leaves all to the nuncio's prudence, and reports that the [330] [577] wanted to see the powers of Benavides.
