## Summary

From the decode with the known key, in a machine transcription. The words are mine.

- Rome has Ceva's letter of the 23rd, with what a cardinal [?Richelieu] told him (codes 330 open, 581 a conjecture).
- S.M. [?] (code 309, a conjecture) proposes to keep to the [?treaty] (code 380, a conjecture) of Regensburg.
- Every act of France for the union will be seen as zeal, not weakness.
