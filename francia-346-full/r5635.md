## Summary

Rome acknowledges letters, one of them of the 21st of the previous month, and a talk with a Father called "Sua Paternità". The letter names an embassy and a declaration on the "[817]sione" of the plenipotentiaries. It says that through "della Carboniera" no effect is to be hoped for now. Several rows read only in fragments.
