- **DECODE:** record R81 ("ASV_i1025_SdS_France_346-8"), https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/81. Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346.
- **Credits:** DECODE record R81 (DECRYPT project) and DECODE's transcription of it; Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346; the key: the published key, as DECODE gives it (see README.md).
- **State:** from key (the key that George Lasry published on DECODE, as DECODE gives it, with 28 three-digit values taken from decipherments of the time and from context, and 2 values corrected from a decipherment of the time). README.md lists each of the 30 values with its source.
- **Read before:** others read these leaves before: the cipher ledger's notes say that George Lasry read R79 to R82, and the ledger itself read R84 in October 2026.
- **A decipherment of the time:** a clerk wrote a decipherment above the cipher rows of some leaves of this series. This page does not copy it.

## Summary

From the decode with the known key. The words are mine.

- Rome, 20 November 1632. Ceva's talk with Richelieu; the union of the crowns; the King's severity against Monsieur's followers (Montmorency beheaded) should go with clemency; France should use its fortune for peace.
