## Summary

Each point ends with its source in brackets. "Decipherment of the time" is the clerk's text between the cipher rows, in a machine transcription. The words are mine. `[?word]` marks a doubtful word.

The clerk's text on this record is spaced letter by letter in many places, and many spans do not read. So this summary has only five short points.

- The writer at Vienna waits to learn how a reply was received at the French court. (decipherment of the time)
- The text names the observance of a capitulation of [?Regensburg]. Who wants to keep to it does not read. (decipherment of the time)
- It names the Electors and the Emperor. It says that the Spaniards [?were called in and] entered Germany. (decipherment of the time)
- The writer asks the addressee to consider one thing. Would it be useful to show the French court how, and in what form, a good friendship with the Emperor could be made now? (decipherment of the time)
- His reason: the Emperor has much to do, so it seems that he can only [?yield]. (decipherment of the time)
