## The leaves

- f. 176r: a covering letter in clear of 5 February, signed by Cardinal Barberini. The added cipher says what he must tell Ceva.
- f. 177r: the cipher letter of 12 February 1633, to the extraordinary nuncio in France.

## Summary

Each point ends with its source: the decode, the decipherment of the time between the rows, or the clear text. All three are machine transcriptions. The words are mine.

- Ceva spoke with prudence about the priory of San Martino. Rome does not want to offend a cardinal [?Richelieu]. (decode; code 581 a conjecture)
- The friendship of others will always prove unfaithful to them: they are without a head and without prudence. (decode; whose friendship is unread)
- Now is the time to press a person (code 577, open), who will hardly agree to restore [130] without need. Rome must persuade him gently. (decode)
- A forecast of Ceva may come true. The Spaniards [?] may make a hold of the French [?] useless. (decode; codes 348 open, 831 a conjecture)
