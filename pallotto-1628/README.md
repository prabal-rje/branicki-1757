# The nuncio Pallotto to Cardinal Barberini, May to August 1628: read with the 1628 key

The papal nuncio at the Emperor's court writes to Cardinal Francesco Barberini, the "Cardinal Padrone", in Rome. DECODE's catalogue names the writer as Pallotto. The despatches come from Innsbruck, Prague and Vienna, from 14 May to 26 August 1628.

**State:** from key (the 1628 key of Norbert Biermann and Thomas Bosbach, March 2018, filed as documents on DECODE record R215). The key is cited here, not copied.

**The cipher:** the digits 1 and 8 are word breaks, two-digit codes are letters and syllables, and three-digit codes are words.

**Read before, and in print.**

- Others solved this cipher before: the key is the work of Norbert Biermann and Thomas Bosbach (2018), filed on DECODE record R215. The cipher ledger (https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver) lists this volume as solved by others, and it reads the volume of 1629 with the same key.
- H. Kiewning, *Nuntiaturberichte aus Deutschland* IV/1, *Nuntiatur des Pallotto 1628* (1895), probably prints many of these despatches. I did not compare this reading with it.

The rows are DECODE's own transcription. DECODE names the transcriber of each record. Each English gloss gives the sense of the letter as far as the decoded rows carry it.

## What the letters say

Their main subject is the war of the Mantuan succession:

- The Emperor's decree on Mantua and Monferrato, his commissioner, and whether the Duke of Nevers will obey.
- The Spanish attack on Casale, and French troops raised to relieve it.
- Plans to give the Duke of Mantua the Cremonese in exchange for Monferrato.
- The claim of Don Ferrante Gonzaga of Guastalla, and the plan to settle it with Reggiolo and nearby villages.
- The Spanish ambassador at court, the Empress, the Emperor's confessor and the Archbishop of Esztergom.
- An imperial army and a Spanish army for Italy, the imperial ban against Nevers, and the papal efforts for peace.

Other letters treat church business:

- The "Jesuitesses" (the English Ladies).
- Orders of the Congregation de Propaganda Fide: the university of Prague and the college of Olomouc.
- Rights of patronage over benefices.

Two letters are in Latin (R264, R272). Nine letters are second copies of others: R238, R241, R245, R249, R252, R254, R257, R259 and R261.
