# R268: the nuncio Pallotto to Cardinal Francesco Barberini, 1628

- **DECODE:** record R268 ("BAV_Barb.lat_6956-53"), https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/268
- **Archive:** Biblioteca Apostolica Vaticana (BAV), Barb. lat. 6956.
- **State:** from key (the 1628 key of Norbert Biermann and Thomas Bosbach, March 2018, filed as documents on DECODE record R215, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/215). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** DECODE's own transcription; DECODE names its transcriber on the record. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## English gloss

Fear that Guastalla or Correggio may be invaded; Pinerolo; troops offered and refused.
