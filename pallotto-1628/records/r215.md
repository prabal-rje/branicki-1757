- **Rows:** DECODE's own transcription; DECODE names its transcriber on the record. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## English gloss

The writer sent part of his household by way of Mantua to learn what Signor Ghibellino brought back from the imperial commissioner. France raises troops (the Duke of Longueville) to force a way through Savoy and relieve Casale. The Marquis of Grana says the Emperor insists that the Duke of Nevers obey.
