# R9899: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, March 1525

- **DECODE:** record R9899, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9899
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 194-195.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

Too little is read to say. Time, place.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 218. Clear words in the rows: 2.
