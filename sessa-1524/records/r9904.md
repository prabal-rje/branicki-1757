# R9904: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, not dated (June 1525?)

- **DECODE:** record R9904, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9904
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 379-382.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

It shares passages with R9905. The mother of the King of France sends an ambassador; the King of England; the Pope; whether they will come; resistance.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 534. Clear words in the rows: 10.
