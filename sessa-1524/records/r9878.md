# R9878: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 18 April 1524 (duplicate of R9877)

- **DECODE:** record R9878, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9878
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 130-131.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Read before:** the cipher ledger read this letter in part before.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

The duplicate of R9877; its rows read poorly. The letters that were not shown, the suspicion, and the talks.
