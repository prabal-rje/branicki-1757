# R9896: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, January 1525

- **DECODE:** record R9896, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9896
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 6-9.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## What it says (English)

The datary and the Pope; the Pope 'is here with the King of France'; people very warm with the datary; the nature of his master; Alberto Pio da Carpi; no provision from the viceroy; the need. Madame was content that the second son of the King of France should have an investiture. The suspicion that was taken; this article; after his letter came, no provision has been made.
