# R9887: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, not dated (between 22 April and May 1524)

- **DECODE:** record R9887, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9887
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 274-277.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

The Pope; it is impossible to secure; the enemy; Siena; someone in France was not very content; matters of Your Majesty. Several code values here are doubtful.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 581. Clear words in the rows: 5.
