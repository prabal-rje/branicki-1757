# R9883: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 22 April 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9883, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9883
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 168-169.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Read before:** the cipher ledger read this letter against its clear text, to build its key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

Most of its rows are masked or do not read. The war will fall on the Pope's shoulders; the French show more contentment; the Cardinal of England, the archbishop of Capua, the Swiss.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 451. Clear words in the rows: 34.
