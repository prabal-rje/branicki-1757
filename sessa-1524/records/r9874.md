# R9874: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 14 April 1524 (last leaf of R9873)

- **DECODE:** record R9874, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9874
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 86-87.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

The last leaf of the letter of 14 April 1524 (R9873): the cipher postscript. Lombardy, the archbishop of Capua, help and money.
