# R9881: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 17 April 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9881, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9881
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 139-140.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Read before:** the cipher ledger read this letter against its clear text, to build its key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

Close to the clerk's decipherment of the time. Sessa has understood that the archbishop of Capua did not leave France content, nor with any of the talks, above all that of peace or truce. Madame the Regent sent a person after him to assure him, so that he would go on with the enterprise; of the one and the other nothing is said. The Pope by no means wants to show displeasure with the French. Of England and of other parts nothing more is known than what Sessa has written.
