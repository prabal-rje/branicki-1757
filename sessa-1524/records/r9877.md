# R9877: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 18 April 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9877, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9877
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 128-129.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Read before:** the cipher ledger read this letter in part before.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## What it says (English)

The Pope's secretary would not show Sessa the letters of the bishop of Veroli. The news agrees with what the bishop wrote to the postmaster. The Swiss, and the talks of peace and truce.
