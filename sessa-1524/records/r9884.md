# R9884: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 22 April 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9884, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9884
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 170-173.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Read before:** the cipher ledger read this letter against its clear text, to build its key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

Close to the clerk's decipherment of the time. All the war will fall on the Pope's shoulders. Neither the Pope nor the Florentines have made more provision of money than Sessa has written. The Cardinal of England and the Regent.
