# R9876: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, not dated; text shared with 14 April 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9876, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9876
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) f. 96.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

A copy of part of the letter of 14 April 1524. The Pope will not declare himself in a league, defensive or offensive. Sessa holds it for certain that, under pressure, the Pope would lean to the French out of fear. Little help, the affairs of Lombardy, and what the archbishop of Capua will bring.
