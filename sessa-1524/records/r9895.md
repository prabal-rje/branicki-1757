# R9895: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 29 July 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9895, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9895
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 510-517.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

Sessa has spoken to the Pope; the enterprise; money; the war; what is necessary.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 284. Clear words in the rows: 14.
