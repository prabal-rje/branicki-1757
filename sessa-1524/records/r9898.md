# R9898: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 24 February 1525

- **DECODE:** record R9898, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9898
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 150-156.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Read before:** the cipher ledger decoded this letter before.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

The Pope and the datary, the datary's attempt to bring the Duke over to the French, money, the Swiss, and the government from here on.

- Cipher units: 3183. Clear words in the rows: 50.
