# R9903: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, not dated (after 29 May 1525)

- **DECODE:** record R9903, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9903
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 351-352.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

The Pope awaits someone's coming; he is very content that nothing has been done in Lombardy; the viceroy's coming is awaited each day; of England no memory; the league; the defence.
