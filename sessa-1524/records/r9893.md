# R9893: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 24 July 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9893, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9893
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 483-485.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Read before:** the cipher ledger read this letter against its clear text, to build its key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

Close to the clerk's decipherment of the time. While he waits for Monsieur de la Roxa (as the clerk spells him), the Pope will not talk of anything that touches the state. There is no mention of peace, truce or war, as if he were not Pope. He will not show himself for one side or the other, so everything is in suspense. Sessa does not know what the Pope will do when that man comes, whom he awaits with desire.
