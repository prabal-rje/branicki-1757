# R9894: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 29 July 1524 (probable)

- **DECODE:** record R9894, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9894
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 504-508.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

Help; the enterprise of France; time; what would be necessary.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 129. Clear words in the rows: 5.
