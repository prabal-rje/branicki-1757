# R9900: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 4 May 1525

- **DECODE:** record R9900, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9900
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 271-272.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

Lombardy and the need; the viceroy; the datary; matters of state; it would be time to come; from England nothing; to enter France; the French would not be acceptable; room given to the enemy; the defence; money; Siena, where the viceroy is.
