# R9905: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, not dated (19 June 1525?)

- **DECODE:** record R9905, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9905
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-34 (9/34) ff. 383-388.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## What it says (English)

It shares passages with R9904. The mother of the King of France sends an ambassador; the archbishop of Capua; the viceroy; money; resistance with the greater part.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 826. Clear words in the rows: 18.
