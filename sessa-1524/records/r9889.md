# R9889: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, 19 May 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9889, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9889
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 320-321.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## What it says (English)

The duplicate of R9890's cipher in part. The Pope shows; the due fulfilment; friendship; the Pope has given no more money; the French.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 384. Clear words in the rows: 5.
