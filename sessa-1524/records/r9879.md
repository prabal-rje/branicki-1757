# R9879: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, April 1524; text shared with 17 April 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9879, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9879
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) f. 135.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

The same cipher passage as R9881. The archbishop of Capua left France not content. In none of the talks, above all those of peace or truce, did things go well. Madame the Regent sent a person after him to assure him. The Pope by no means wants to show displeasure towards the French. Nothing more is known from England or elsewhere.
