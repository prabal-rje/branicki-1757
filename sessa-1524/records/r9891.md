# R9891: the Duke of Sessa to Charles V, June 1524

- **DECODE:** record R9891, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/9891
- **Archive:** Real Academia de la Historia, Colección Salazar y Castro A-31 (9/31) ff. 361-364.
- **State:** from key (the cipher ledger's key, with more values from clear texts of the same letters; see `../README.md`). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What it says (English)

The nuncio; the archbishop of Capua; a suspicion; help to someone in France; no money given; the datary; Alberto Pio da Carpi; the viceroy; the war.

## Numbers

- Cipher units: 449. Clear words in the rows: 18.
