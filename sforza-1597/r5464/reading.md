**These are partial reads from the ciphertext alone.** No key sheet and no clear text checks them. `check.md` gives what supports them and what does not.

The ledger's maintainer read fragments of this letter first, for example "sempre desiderat amici" and "facto stamatina tornare".

**Marks.**

- The Italian is our reading of the decode in `decryption.txt`. It writes "v" where the decode has "u".
- Letters in [brackets] are letters that we supply.
- [?] marks a doubtful word. Where we change a shown letter, the shown form follows, for example (shown `napole`).
- `...` leaves out letters that do not read.
- Page 1 line n is line n of the ledger's token file. Page 2 line n is its line 50 + n.

## What the stretches speak of

- friendship, goodwill and an alliance by marriage;
- a league for the defence of the states, in which the Pope takes part;
- troops of each side, bound to serve the other;
- the secrecy of the writings;
- the king;
- a return to Milan.
