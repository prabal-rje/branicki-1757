# Francia 346: the names, phrases and stretches that read

DECODE, records R74 to R78, R5626, R5635, R5636, R5637, R5640, R5643, R5647 and R5648, and in the appendix R5642, R5645, R5646, R5649, R5650 and R5651 (Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 346). Italian, in cipher.

Read from the ciphertext alone. I made these readings before the check against the published key. The last column of each table gives the check, made afterwards. `check.md` gives the method.

**Marks.**

- A label such as `07_102r_R77_P1 C15` is DECODE record R77, page 1 (folio 102r), row 15. The same labels lead each line of `decryption.txt`.
- The "Decoded" column quotes the decode exactly, with its gap marks. `·` is a gap.
- The "Restored" column is my conjecture. Square brackets mark every letter or word that I add, including double letters. `…` leaves out letters that do not read.
- The decode often writes a single consonant where Italian has a double one.
