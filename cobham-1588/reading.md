# Readings: Lord Cobham to Sir Francis Walsingham (May to June 1588)

Clear secretary hand is in roman type. Decoded cipher words are in **bold**. Genuine open or uncertain words stay bracketed `[?]`. Contemporary number codes from glossed siblings (`R8488`, `R8489`, `R8493`): `7` = Queen Elizabeth I, `15` = Duke of Parma, `22` = Sir James Croft, `27` = Dr. Valentine Dale, square = the States.

## 1. DECODE R8495 (Harley MS 287, f. 92r, "Belgia", 27 May 1588)

Resolved: 35 of 38 cipher words (92.1%).

Clear opening reports that a ship from "Alaveilo" brings word that the Spanish Armada is still at Lisbon and is expected to sail to Coruna in Galicia and pass along the coast, and that 200 Hamburg mariners from Sluys have returned to Dunkirk.

Decoded espionage block (lines 12 to 22):

> **[Jemis? Lighmen?]** and **Edward Jonson** seent by yor **[?]** are imprisoned **in Bridges [Bruges]**. It ys reported that they have **charged Stanleys liftenant** of receyte of **letters**, wheruppon he hathe byn condemned and executed **on Monday last [by that / be shot?] ner Dismuyde [Dixmude]**. That daye was **ther [ex]ecutid an ancient [ensign] of Newport, for writing of versis to thee preist**. **Captein Boyce** ys also **inprisoned at Bridgis**, the cawse ys not knowen: Of latte ther are com to **Stanley** som **Irish noble men**.

Open items (3 of 38): first courier name (`I 7 Y I A | L I G h Y 7 N`), 4-sign word after "seent by yor" (`G L c A`), and 6-sign phrase before "ner Dismuyde" (`B X A h c A`).

## 2. DECODE R8496 (Harley MS 287, f. 96v, "Binborow" [Bourbourg], 9 June 1588)

Resolved: 28 of 30 cipher words (93.3%).

> ...**the Erle of Westmorland passed by**, ys himse[lf] newe arryved **within France [servis?]** o[f] theyr comynge hat[h] informed yow, of **Roper** and of his **servant Ihon preist**, a deller in **treason** [or *treaters*], and a carier **of letters for the Ladi Wauce [Lady Vaux]**, and that privily ... that he had **written into England** ys fynlay [?] ... and rather **crafti traters [treaters/traitors]**, theyr **witte**, they can shet a good face, how **falce so ever** yt ys / I pray let me know by yor nexte whether yow have receyd the cypher that I sent yow: what order yow have geven for **Captan [Barrin...?]** I pray let me here from yow: he woold doo much good servis.

Plain English postscript at foot of f. 96v (independent corroboration of `L = p` in *Roper*):

> ...with him cometh one **Roper**, yt ys thought here that her ma[jesty] doo revoke us home: for all our frendes and followers are very hevy.

Open items (2 of 30): surname of `Captan [B Q V V I N r X]` and exact grammatical form of `A 7 V u c A` after "within France".

## 3. DECODE R8492 (Harley MS 287, f. 88r, 1588, undated)

Resolved: 40 of 55 cipher words (72.7%).

> Ther ys captayne Mongomery gone of latt ynto Schotland. **[E01a] [E01b]** w^t 40 men of **acont** have promised **the haven** of **Rainsport [?]** yn **[E06a]** **ther** to **[15: Parma]**: of latte ther ys comyng of 30 **[E07a-b]** whoo have **a gret pention monthly**. Ther **be two Italian[s] starving** [or *serving*] **at [E09e-f]**, the ony [one] **is namid Antonio**, the other **[E11]**, they be booth **[E12]** yn good **[E13]**; they have promised they [wold] **deliver [E14b?] the ton [town]** to **[15: Parma]** by menes of our aboade. Here are comyng of others hethere **divers** [on **E15b**] **soldiers are allured** away to the **number of** fortty; of latt they have suche **large promises made** unto theyr 500 **entised [E19b]** whoo have w^t them two or fyve more / our aboade dothe no good here, such ys the goyng up and down; and such **intelligens** ther ys in the **enemys** **[15: Parma]** **[E21b-c]** to **[E22a-b]** **in France** ... that he wyll have **a lisur [leisure]** to deale w^t hym ... **[Twing/Ewing?]** and **Winter** woolde send mony to theyr wyfes, yt ys desired to be knowen whether yt maye be taken and sent over.

Open items (15 of 55): rare proper names and clusters containing the unglossed sign `K` (`E01a-b`, `E06a`, `E07a-b`, `E09e-f`, `E11` to `E13`, `E15b`, `E19b`, `E21b-c`, `E22a-b`, `E27` to `E28`).

## 4. DECODE R8490 (Harley MS 287, ff. 80r to 80v, Ostend, 5 May 1588)

Resolved: 39 of 43 cipher words (90.7%).

New resolutions beyond Brice Dudout's 9 November pass:

- `C05c` (`D 7 Y N O E`): **demande** ("yt ys honorable for [7: the Queen] to **demande** ther surti to these termes"), unlocked by `Y = m`.
- `C06` and `C07` (`A V 7 A T A` / `A V 7 A T`): **treates** and **treat** ("as usuall yn all **treates** ... at the **treat** of **Breda**"), confirming `T = e`.
- `C13a` to `C13c` (`A d N | d F | X c V I w 7`): **ton [or son] of Yorike [York/Yorke]**, linked to `C14` to `C16`: "has a **brother** about **[15: Parma]** who promiseth his mayster **Rainsport** a **haven** to open" (the same 9-letter haven code `V Q I N A L c V A` as in `R8492 E05a-b`).

Open items (4 of 43): `C01` (`c V N 8 S`), `C02d` (`L U N 8 7 A`), `C03g` (`X d + c u 8 7`), and the exact geographical identification of `Rainsport`.
