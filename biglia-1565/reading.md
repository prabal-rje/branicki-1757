# Biglia to Rome, December 1565 (letter 2): the stretches that read

Milan, Veneranda Biblioteca Ambrosiana, Trotti 22, letter 2: fol. 7r, lines 33 to 45, and fol. 7v, lines 1 to 23. Italian, in cipher. No DECODE record is known for this letter.

Read from the ciphertext alone. I made these readings before the check against Brice Dudout's reading. The check compared letters, not these readings one by one. `check.md` gives the check.

**Marks.**

- The Italian quotes the shown read of `decryption.txt`. ` / ` is a line end; the words run on across lines.
- Letters in [brackets] fill a gap of the shown read from the best start (the second part of `decryption.txt`). On artificial test letters, about one letter in seven of the best start is wrong.
- `…` leaves out letters that do not read.
- The spelling is the decode's: "fato" for "fatto" (done), "adeso" for "adesso" (now).

The letter speaks in the first person. It joins words of devotion and affection with numbers of troops and the king. This describes my read, not the letter's content.

## Names, places and dates

- **Dates:** "di ·ecembre del" (7v 23), of December. The best start gives "fecembre": d and f write the same digit. "ieri" (yesterday, 7v 11).
- **Titles and persons:** "el re" (the king, 7r 45). No personal name reads.
- **Places:** none reads.
- **Troops:**
  - "milia fanti" (7r 45);
  - "[d]ue mila cauali" (7v 1);
  - "li capi di ss[e] fanti" (7v 3 to 4);
  - "li fanti" and "li cauali" (7v 4).
