# The state of every collection

This page gives the state of every collection of cipher letters that I worked on, as of 19 November 2026. A unit is a page, a record or a case, as the table says.

The states:

- **From key:** the reading uses a key that exists (a key of the time, a printed key or another person's key), or a decipherment of the time.
- **From ciphertext:** the key came from the cipher text alone.
- **Not read yet:** no reading yet.
- **Too small:** too few signs.
- **No cipher text:** no cipher rows, or no saved text.

**558 of 775 units have a reading:** 349 from key and 209 from ciphertext. The units differ by collection: pages, records, letters or cases. Some of these readings are counted but not shown in this repository; the last list below names them. The single letters of the front page, the Morosini despatches and the Biglia letter are not in this table; the front page gives their state. The nine early Sforza records are in both.

Notes on the table:

- **Sforza, from key:** 14 pages are read on my own transcription: 5 with the archive's key sheet (DECODE record R5347) and 9 with the cipher ledger's keys. Of the 5, R5581 page 2 is not shown: its 54 signs are too few to be sure. Seven more pages are read with the ledger's key on the ledger's own rows only. That is the ledger's work.
- **Botti, no state:** 5 pages with dots. Their reading is not sure, and this repository does not show them.
- **Pallotto, too small:** R219 is not read; its heading says 1629.
- **Francia 346, the early leaves:** R83 uses another cipher. Only the clerk's decipherment above its rows reads. My record counts its page as from key; this repository gives no reading of it.
- **Francia 346, the later records, from key:** 31 records with the published key, and 13 records in another cipher with a key taken from the clerk's decipherment (R5634, R5638, R5639, R5660 to R5666, R5669 to R5671). For ten of the 13, `francia-346-full/` says what each letter says.

## What stays out of this repository

- **Readings that rest on a clerk's decipherment:** the Barras letter (DECODE record R2856; Beinecke Library, GEN MSS 146), Francia 346 R83, and the 13 Francia 346 records in another cipher, beyond what `francia-346-full/` already says.
- **Readings with the cipher ledger's key:** of the Sforza pages, the ledger's work. `sforza-160-records/README.md` lists them with their state.

DECODE, the database of historical ciphers of the DECRYPT project, holds the records named here. Its record pages: https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/ followed by the record number.

DECODE's images are for research use only. They may not be redistributed or published without permission from the archive that holds the document. No DECODE image is in this repository, and I copy no transcription file of DECODE. The cipher rows in the `transcription/` folders of Botti, Cosimo I, the 160 Sforza records, Sessa and Francia 32 are my own transcription.
