# R5559, page 1

- **DECODE:** R5559, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5559
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598.
- **Pages:** page 1 (read together with R5563)
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

The writer reports on an "enterprise" and on "Pierantonio", who meant to advise the lord in detail; he waits for an answer from Naples and asks that the lord be told all that has passed.
