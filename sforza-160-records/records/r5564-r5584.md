# R5564, R5565, R5582, R5583 and R5584 (read together)

- **DECODE:** R5564, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5564; R5565, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5565; R5582, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5582; R5583, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5583; R5584, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5584
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598.
- **Pages:** page 1 of each; of R5582, rows 1 to 16 only
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Doubt:** this reading is less sure than most pages of this folder.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

One key reads these pages together. The rows do not mark where one page ends and the next begins; the lines follow the order of the pages.
