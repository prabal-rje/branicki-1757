# R5453, pages 1 and 2 (read together)

- **DECODE:** R5453, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5453
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1597.
- **Pages:** pages 1 and 2
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Year:** most likely 1491 (30 November). Reason: the letter calls the marriage concluded and the bride "queen of France". Anne of Brittany was engaged to Charles VIII on 17 November 1491 and married him on 6 December 1491. The year rests on this one event. Source: en.wikipedia, "Anne of Brittany", https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anne_of_Brittany.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

One key reads these pages together. The rows do not mark where one page ends and the next begins; the lines follow the order of the pages.

## Gloss

A report from a king's side: envoys of the Swiss League; a duchy for the duke of Orléans; Bourges. The marriage is held as concluded, and the bride has herself called queen of France. Dated "the last of November".
