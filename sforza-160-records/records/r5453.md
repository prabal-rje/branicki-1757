# R5453, pages 1 and 2 (read together)

- **DECODE:** R5453, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5453
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1597.
- **Pages:** pages 1 and 2
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.
