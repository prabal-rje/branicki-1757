- **DECODE:** R5523, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5523
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598.
- **Pages:** page 1 (read together with R5520 and R5541)
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

"Things here are in great confusion" at the coming of someone of the Pope; men given by the King; the said Iacomo is to be in the pay of the Church. Dated in June.
