## Gloss

"Yesterday morning I wrote": the coming of an ambassador; the writer's son; a sick man asked to go; a courier sent to his king; the ambassador came "by the work of" someone, hoping for a settlement.

The same letter: a resolution taken, the reputation of the parties, a proposal to put off the "fact of arms" for one year. Dated the nineteenth of February.
