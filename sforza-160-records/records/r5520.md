# R5520, page 1

- **DECODE:** R5520, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5520
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598.
- **Pages:** page 1 (read together with R5523 and R5541)
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

Ferrante, the council, a "Giambattista" secretary of the King of Aragon, and "Iacomo Picenino" (perhaps Jacopo Piccinino), a soldier of the Church against an adversary; the Florentines are not to send their men.
