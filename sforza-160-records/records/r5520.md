# R5520, page 1

- **DECODE:** R5520, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5520
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598.
- **Pages:** page 1 (read together with R5523 and R5541)
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Year:** 1465 or before; most likely between 1455 and June 1458. Reason: Jacopo Piccinino died in 1465, and a living "King of Aragon" fits Alfonso V, who died on 27 June 1458. Source: S. Ferente, "Piccinino, Jacopo", *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani*, vol. 83 (2015).
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## Gloss

A "Giambattista", secretary of the King of Aragon, sent here by the king; the council. "Iacomo Picenino" (perhaps Jacopo Piccinino) is to be put in pay, perhaps of the Church, against an adversary. The Florentines are not to send their men. The rows do not show the name "Ferrante" clearly.
