- **DECODE:** R5416, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5416
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1597.
- **Pages:** page 1 (read together with R5456)
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Doubt:** this reading is less sure than most pages of this folder.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

The writer reports on Ludovico, who is to contract with a lady, and names a "Hieronimo" (perhaps of Forli). He speaks of "good terms" with the consent of the lord, of "hopes of news in Italy" that keep "the friend" in suspense, of the mother, comforted to wait, and of the son. The page ends with a date in April.
