- **Pages:** page 1 (read together with R5416)
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Doubt:** this reading is less sure than most pages of this folder.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

The writer sends notices to the governor: the lord and his army are back; a citizen named Nicolo, known as a man of an archbishop, is named. Ludovico would go "in person" if the restitution of the citadel is delayed further. Dated the eleventh of September.
