# R5541, page 1

- **DECODE:** R5541, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5541
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598.
- **Pages:** page 1 (read together with R5520 and R5523)
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

The Venetians, men of esteem, a "messer Petro"; the King of Aragon; a term of eight months.
