# R5563, pages 1 and 2

- **DECODE:** R5563, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5563
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598.
- **Pages:** pages 1 and 2 (read together with R5559)
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Italian
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

About the chapters of an agreement and a restitution; the duke, the "enterprise", and an accord.

The same matter: the duke, the obligation of the chapters, someone come to Milan. Dated in December.
