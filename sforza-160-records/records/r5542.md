# R5542, page 1 (Latin)

- **DECODE:** R5542, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5542
- **Archive:** Archivio di Stato di Milano, Carteggio Sforzesco, cartella 1598.
- **Pages:** page 1
- **State:** from ciphertext (the key of R5521 page 1). Not checked against a key.
- **Language:** Latin
- **Year:** most likely 1492; in any case between 1490 and October 1494. Reason: the pope is ill in the letter, and Innocent VIII died on 25 July 1492. On 2 July 1492 Milan also urged that the bishop of Nitra be sent into Italy. The range: the bishop of Nitra took office in 1490, and Ludovico was duke of Bari until October 1494. Sources: L. Pastor, *The History of the Popes*, on Innocent VIII; A. Berzeviczy, *Aragoniai Beatrix magyar királyné életére vonatkozó okiratok* (Budapest, 1914), no. CLXVII.
- **Doubt:** this reading is less sure than most pages of this folder.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

A Latin letter in the name of Ludovico Maria Sforza. It answers a letter, addresses "dominatio vestra" and "maiestas", and names the lord of Nitra, his coming into Italy, a treaty and promptness.

### English text

This English follows the decoded rows. It is read from ciphertext, and no key checks it. `[?]` marks a stretch that does not read. A word in square brackets with a question mark is a doubtful reading. A word in square brackets without one fills hidden signs where the word is plain. This page is less sure than most.

This only remains: the lord of Nitra must be sent into Italy without delay. The other things that concern us will be ready. [Duplicate?]. May Your Lordship fare well. Milan, the twentieth of July. Ludovicus Maria Sfortia Vicecomes, duke of Bari.
