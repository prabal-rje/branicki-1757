# R5614: Filippo Sega, nuncio in Paris, to Rome (ff. 047r, 048r)

- **DECODE:** record R5614, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5614
- **Archive:** Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 32.
- **State:** from key (A. Meister's printed key no. 65, 1906, "cifra servita in legatione sub Sisto V"). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## Gloss

Sega, Paris, 9 January 1591 (ff. 47-48). Read in part: confusion, soldiery, discredit, the politiques, the death of a chevalier, a return, Saint-Denis.
