# R5612: Filippo Sega, nuncio in Paris, to Rome (ff. 017r, 017v)

- **DECODE:** record R5612, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/5612
- **Archive:** Archivio Apostolico Vaticano, Segreteria di Stato, Francia 32.
- **State:** from key (A. Meister's printed key no. 65, 1906, "cifra servita in legatione sub Sisto V"). The key is cited here, not copied.
- **Read before:** Brice Dudout read f. 17 of this record before, with the same key. His reading is in the cipher ledger.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## Gloss

Sega, Paris, 26 November 1590: the imprisoned bishop leans to the opposite party; Sega will temporise.
