# Francia 32: Filippo Sega, nuncio in Paris, to Rome, 1590 to 1591

Filippo Sega, bishop of Piacenza and nuncio in Paris, writes to Rome during the war of the League. Most of each letter is in clear. The cipher passages carry his judgements.

**State:** from key (A. Meister's printed key no. 65, 1906, "cifra servita in legatione sub Sisto V"). The key is cited here, not copied.

**Result:** two letters carry a decipherment of the time in the same record. Of the decoded letters, .854 (R5613) and .848 (R5621) match it.

- **Read before.** Others read part of this file before, with the same key: Brice Dudout read ff. 17 and 71 (records R5612 and R5616) and, in part, ff. 49v to 54v (record R5615). His readings are in the cipher ledger.
- **Read at the time.** Two letters carry a decipherment of the time in the same record (R5613, R5621).
- The rows come from my own transcription of the DECODE images.
- Each English gloss gives the sense of the letter as far as the decoded rows carry it.

## What the letters say

- **R5612, 26 November 1590.** The imprisoned bishop leans to the opposite party (Navarre's). Sega will temporise.
- **R5613, 9 January 1591.** Lignerac goes, on Mayenne's order, to Poitou and Brittany.
- **R5614, 9 January 1591 (ff. 47 to 48).** Read in part: confusion, the soldiery, discredit, the politiques, the death of a chevalier, a return, Saint-Denis.
- **R5615, early January 1591.** The duplicate of a long letter (ff. 49v to 54v).
- **R5616, ff. 61v to 63v.** Read in part: Nemours and his son, a wife, troops of a thousand, six hundred and two hundred, a garrison, help promised.
- **R5616, 16 January 1591 (f. 71).** Mayenne wrote well of Sega to Rome.
- **R5617, March 1591 (f. 107r).** Read in part: governed, merchants.
- **R5621, 21 December 1590.** The agreement at Tours may fail. La Châtre withdraws, because Mayenne did not keep his promises. The Duchess of Guise is angry about her son's release.
