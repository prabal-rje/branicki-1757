## Historical content (seven months after Lutzen)

1. **Saxon envoy Friedrich Lebzelter's defection overture (P3 Latin postscript):** "Lebzelter arrived here today from Hamburg; the **son-in-law** (`gener`) of this better [official `828`] whispered **in my ear** (`in aurem`) that he is demanding money and urging a **total separation from Sweden** (`totalem af Swerige separationem`) or at least [`2523`m]."
2. **Lubeck's refusal to send condolences on Gustavus Adolphus's death (P1, point 4):** Salvius reports an insider tip that Lubeck's leaders privately debated whether to **condole** (`condolera`) with Salvius on the king's death and voted no so as not to **compromise/blacken themselves** (`denigrera` sigh) with `[2066 1/2]`.
3. **Anti-Swedish counterweight coalition (P1, point 3):** Salvius reports a plan to draw Holstein (`1917`) and `[880, 881]` into a **balance** against Sweden (`3143`), while local leaders seek **delay** (`dilation`) and remain **ambiguous** (`ambiguum`), urging `[2320]` to **forestall** (`prevenire`) `[Oo. och O.]`.
