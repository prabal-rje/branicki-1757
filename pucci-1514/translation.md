> Magnificent Lord and my Patron, on the 25th of this month I arrived at the court of the Catholic King, much honoured by His Highness, and on Sunday the 27th I had a most gracious audience from him, in which I communicated all that Our Lord [the Pope] had charged me with.

## Clear closing

> Leaving on Saturday, which will be 2 September, I hope within a few days to be in Lisbon. I expect to send the Magnificent Lord a horse of beauty and quality... gifted by the Count of Almazan... I have given commission and orders to buy four fine colts from the mountains of Alcaraz, two for Your Lordship and two for the Magnificent Lord... and because I know how much he delights in music, I am having an instrument made for him at Zaragoza like the one Santa Croce gave the Pope, which plays three or four [voices]... Valladolid, the last day of August 1514. Your servant, Antonio Pucci.
