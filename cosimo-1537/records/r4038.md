# R4038: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 4 March 1538

- **DECODE:** record R4038, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4038
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_28.
- **State:** from key (the archive's key table, DECODE record R4033, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4033). The table is cited here, not copied. The rows show only the table's own values.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## What the cipher passages say (my words)

Niccolini is to accept the extension, as they have said. He is to seek a better condition. Any further advantage is left to him.
