# R4049: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 28 February 1539

- **DECODE:** record R4049, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4049
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_107.
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What the cipher passage says

The writer sends a short note to say where someone is at present. The read names "Signor Stefano da Palestrina", one "Ugolino", a journey to Pisa and "the ambassadors". The rest is too broken for a summary.

## A reading with English

- "scriuo questa poli[z]a a uostra …" — "I write this note to your …".
- "… doue al presen[t]e si tro[ua] … il [signor] Stefano [d]a P[al]estrina" — "… where at present … Signor Stefano da Palestrina is".
- "… che mi fara pi[ac]ere …" — "… which will give me pleasure …".
- "Ugolino … [f]ermat[o] …" — "Ugolino … stopped …".
- "… [d]ipoi [a]ndra [a] Pisa … li ambas[c]iadori … partir[e]" — "… then he will go to Pisa … the ambassadors … to leave".
