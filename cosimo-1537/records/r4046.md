# R4046: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 17 November 1538

- **DECODE:** record R4046, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4046
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_74.
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What the cipher passages say

The read shows two rows. They ask someone to watch "the movements, orders and plans" of certain people, and name a "messer".

## The rows as read

One line for each cipher row of the transcription. `·` is a code that the read does not show. `[?]` is a clear word, or a stretch that does not read.

- image 1: `ad·nuigilarelianda`
- image 1: `ment·ordini·disegnid·cotes`
- image 1: `toro [?] tene·sobene`
- image 1: `decitatomeser·inoldoda`
- image 1: `bino`

## A reading with English

- "ad [i]nuigilare li andamenti, ordini [e] disegni d[i] co[s]te[s]toro" — "to watch over the movements, orders and plans of these people".
- "… so bene … meser … " — "… I know well … messer …".
