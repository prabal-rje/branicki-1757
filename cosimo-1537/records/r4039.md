# R4039: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 4 October 1538

- **DECODE:** record R4039, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4039
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_54.
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What the cipher passages say

Cosimo writes about a marriage alliance and "the matter of the wife". These points come from a partial read:

- His intention in the matter of the wife has always been the same, from the start until now.
- He encloses a copy of a letter. Its aim is to cut off this negotiation entirely, because it is too contrary to his mind.
- He warns Niccolini about one man. That man is not to be trusted, and he fears to lose "his penitentiary" (an office).
- The same man acted against Duke Alessandro "of good memory", and against Cosimo at his arrival.
- Cosimo was ready to speak of a wife only as far as "His Majesty" commanded.
- He asks Niccolini to remedy the harm as best he can. He does not want "the folly of others" to harm him.

## A reading with English

The word cuts and the letters in brackets are mine. Words in brackets stand for hidden codes.

- "del parentad[o] … rincrescemi m[olto] … e la angusti[a ch]e fanno, nel quale io ui [ho] ueduto per il tenore delle lettere uostre" — "of the marriage alliance … it grieves me much … and the distress that they cause, in which I have seen you, by the tenor of your letters".
- "pure mi sono p[e]rsuaso che i sapere uoi la intentione [e] deliberatione mia nel caso della moglie, la quale dal primo insino al pres[ente] giorno sempre è stata la medesima" — "yet I am persuaded that you know my intention and my decision in the matter of the wife. From the start until the present day it has always been the same."
- "… cosa alcuna in questo mondo potere … disporre di me" — "… anything in this world could … dispose of me".
- "il conoscere la [n]at[u]r[a] del detto meglio an[c]ora di me ui [h]abbi leuato assai di dispiacere" — "that you know the nature of the said man even better than I do has spared you much displeasure".
- "in questa lettera scriuo al detto … quanto uede[re]te per la inclusa copia, per tagliare totalmente questa pratica, come troppo contrari[a] … solo alla mente [e] intenti[one mia]" — "in this letter I write to the said man … as you will see by the enclosed copy, to cut off this negotiation entirely, as too contrary … to my mind and intention".
- "diciate [e] mostriate [non] essere da fidarsi ne da [fon]darsi sopra le parole de[l] … [q]uale [la] natura sua tale quale u[o]i sapete, per il timor[e] che [h]a di perdere quella sua penitentiaria" — "say and show that one must not trust or build on the words of … whose nature is such as you know, for the fear that he has to lose that penitentiary of his".
- "il desiderio di guadagnarsi …" — "the desire to win himself …".
- "di metere in pratica tutto quello che g[li] uenisse sognato … al passar suo di qua" — "to put into practice all that he dreamed up … when he passed through here".
- "che di moglie ero per parlarne … quanto sua maestà comandassi, ma in quel tempo ne anco per pensarui, essend[o] a termine nelle altre pratiche che io fussi ridotto [i]n necessita di pensare a [a]ltro" — "that of a wife I would speak … as far as His Majesty commanded, but at that time not even to think of it, because the other negotiations had brought me to the need to think of other things".
- "insomma feci ogni cosa per tagliare … [ragio]namento [e] leuargli tal pratica di mano" — "in short I did everything to cut off … the talk, and to take that negotiation out of his hands".
- "una cosa simile aspettauo da lu[i], sapendo quello [ch]e haueua fatto al duca Alessandro bona memoria, [e r]icordando … quello [ch]e haueua fatto a me nella sua arriuata" — "I expected such a thing from him, because I knew what he had done to Duke Alessandro of good memory, and I remembered what he had done to me at his arrival".
- "insomma quella è [un]a natura tanto inc[on]siderata che piu [n]o[n] si puo imaginare … credo come molti che le sia malignita" — "in short, it is a nature so thoughtless that one cannot imagine more … I believe, as many do, that it is malice".
- "[h]auete la mia uolunta: andate remediando in quel miglior modo che uoi saperete [e] potrete a questo inconueniente, ricordandoui che io [non] uoglio che la pa[zz]ia d'altri nuoca a me in modo alcuno" — "you have my will: go on and remedy this trouble in the best way that you know and can, and remember that I do not want the folly of others to harm me in any way".
- "… essendo andato tanto innanzi … [senza] commissione alcuna, uoglio che torni a dietro piu tosto …" — "… since he has gone so far … without any commission, I want him to go back rather …".
