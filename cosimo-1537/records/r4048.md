# R4048: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 9 February 1539

- **DECODE:** record R4048, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4048
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_104.
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What the cipher passages say

The read shows two rows. They name "those ministers" and "Francesco Valori".

## The rows as read

One line for each cipher row of the transcription. `·` is a code that the read does not show. `[?]` is a clear word, or a stretch that does not read.

- image 3: `diiquei·inistriceba`
- image 3: `reii·de···disad·toiiaco·o`
- image 3: `difra`
- image 3: `ncesco·alori`

## A reading with English

- "di quei [m]inistri …" — "of those ministers …".
- "… di Francesco [V]alori" — "… of Francesco Valori".
