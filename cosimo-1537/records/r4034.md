# R4034: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 17 December 1537

- **DECODE:** record R4034, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4034
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_02.
- **State:** from key (the archive's key table, DECODE record R4033, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4033). The table is cited here, not copied. The rows show only the table's own values.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## What the cipher passages say (my words)

The tithes. Cosimo asks that the state be spared for now, as it is exhausted by such an exaction. The King of France, His Majesty's help, the Emperor's favour and the Emperor's mind toward Italy are named. Niccolini is to draw the matter out as long as he can, with no offer, because time can bring much advantage.
