# R4047: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 18 January 1539

- **DECODE:** record R4047, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4047
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_99.
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What the cipher passages say

Cosimo writes about a "motu proprio" (an act that the pope issues on his own initiative) and about how to use it. These points come from a partial read:

- The matter "would turn to our great advantage, more than theirs".
- He hopes that they will do it, and he says that it is right to act "at the right time and place".
- He wants to make them feel "a great suspicion".
- When "this fear" is taken away, "another suspicion" stays "in this cause".

## A reading with English

The word cuts and the letters in brackets are mine.

- "… el motu [p]roprio … di farlo" — "… the motu proprio … to do it".
- "questo tornereb[b]e in grandi[ssimo] … comodo nostro piu che loro" — "this would turn to the great … advantage of us more than of them".
- "… opinione e da cred[er]e che lo faran[n]o" — "… opinion, and it is to be believed that they will do it".
- "… in simili casi … questa … [l]or[o] uolunt[à] … aiuto" — "… in such cases … this … their will … help".
- "pero sar[e]b[b]e bene che … a proposito a tempo [e] luogo" — "so it would be good that … at the right time and place".
- "motu proprio noi … ca[c]ciati … lo trouando" — "motu proprio, we … driven out … finding it".
- "insom[m]a fare in modo che penetras[s]i lo[ro] un gran sospet[t]o" — "in short, act so that a great suspicion reaches them".
- "… leuataci la paura di questo, ci rest[a] altro sospet[t]o … in quest[a] causa" — "… once the fear of this is taken from us, another suspicion stays … in this cause".
