# R4041: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 10 October 1538

- **DECODE:** record R4041, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4041
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_59.
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## What the cipher passages say

The read shows short pieces only. They name "this negotiation", "the matter of Giulio", and "ridiculous and frivolous causes".

## A reading with English

- "al[la] pratic[a] del … questa pratica del …" — "to the negotiation of … this negotiation of …".
- "… paia cosa mendicata … caso del [G]iulio" — "… may seem a begged thing … the matter of Giulio".
- "… si ridicule [e] friuole cause per lui ad …" — "… such ridiculous and frivolous causes for him to …".
