# R4042: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 28 October 1538

- **DECODE:** record R4042, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4042
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_64.
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What the cipher passages say

The read shows short pieces only. They name "Don Giovanni", an examination, men "sent to Spain", one "Antonio", news ("ragguaglio") and "a most noble person".

## A reading with English

- "a don Giouan[ni] … che si esamina … il che si è fatto" — "to Don Giovanni … which is being examined … which has been done".
- "… mandat[i] [i]n Spagna" — "… sent to Spain".
- "… esser Antonio …" — "… messer Antonio …".
- "raguag[li]o … di tutto questo" — "news … of all this".
- "… nobilissima persona" — "… a most noble person".
