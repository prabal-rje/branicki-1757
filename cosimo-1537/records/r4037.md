# R4037: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 16 February 1538

- **DECODE:** record R4037, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4037
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_23.
- **State:** from key (the archive's key table, DECODE record R4033, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4033). The table is cited here, not copied. The rows show only the table's own values.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images, in `../transcription/`. `../TRANSCRIPTION.md` explains its marks.

## What the cipher passages say (my words)

The two papal tithes on Florence. Cosimo will act as Prince Doria acts for the city of Genoa. A proposal: to be content with half of the two tithes, one raised now and one a year later, by a Florentine commissioner whom His Holiness appoints. Church censures and an interdict are named.
