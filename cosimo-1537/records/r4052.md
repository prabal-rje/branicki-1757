# R4052: Cosimo I de' Medici to Agnolo Niccolini, 22 May 1539

- **DECODE:** record R4052, https://de-crypt.org/decrypt-web/RecordsView/4052
- **Archive:** Florence, Archivio Niccolini di Camugliano, Fondo antico 214, inserto 1. DECODE's image name: 214_1_129.
- **State:** from ciphertext. Not checked against a key.
- **Rows:** my own transcription of the DECODE images. The cipher rows themselves are not in this repository.

## What the cipher passages say

The read shows a few letters and short words only. The cipher passages of this letter are short, and many codes stay hidden. No content is claimed. One phrase reads "un grande accidente" ("a great accident"; not sure).
