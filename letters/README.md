## What they say, in short

**1398, dated 24 July.** A field report on the Russian army under Apraxin near the frontier of [625], likely Prussia. Town deputies come to submit, carts and men are taken without pay, and a rumour says flour and bread were poisoned.

**1399.** Faction politics in Lithuania: Grodno, Zdzięcioł, Wilno, a crisis, and a new secret correspondence.

**1400, from Nieśwież, in September.** A bloody election assembly at Brest, massacred gentlemen, brother against brother, and a rival party spreading money across the palatinates.
