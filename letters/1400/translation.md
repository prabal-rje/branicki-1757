Nieśwież, 11 September [824] [811] [823] [818] [810]

Sir,

Every day we hear here very tragic news of the dietines of our [490]. Whichever way one turns, one meets only the corpses of gentlemen massacred at these riotous assemblies, which are carried to the courts and to the [740] to be presented there: the deadly result of this hatred that is tearing apart the [840].

This time at Brest of [491], for electing the deputies to the general [280], there was a dietine of the most riotous kind. Everything there breathed only rage, fury and carnage. Brother against brother, kin against kin, were let loose with the utmost inhumanity.

Count Sapieha, the [575], was there to keep good order, and to try to bring about a peaceful choice of deputies in this palatinate who could serve our aims. But alas, this lord, whose protests made not the least impression on the furious nobles, is lucky to have escaped their hands safe and sound.

All of them, except a very small number, were of the opposite party, which arbitrarily elected as deputies Mr Sobiemblowski and Subin, the lieutenant of Mr Sosnowski, who was struck down by the known decree. Apparently this was to get the [820] [380] to relax somewhat the severity of the sentence passed against him, although this election, being illegal, will not hold.

A dozen gentlemen, who will not fail to go to Warsaw as well to block the work of these illegally elected deputies, have solemnly protested against this act at the court of Bielsk in Podlachia.

At the head of the mutineers was Mr [?Buchowiecki], territorial notary of that palatinate, once one of our friends, but who has since turned his coat. At this dietine he made plenty of work for Mr the [576].

This troublemaker still claims he will bring this palatinate to a review [385] of the nobles, the first step toward a confederation. But Mr Wereszczaka, ensign of this palatinate, backed by Mr the [577], strongly opposes it.

All this uproar comes from the generosity of the opposite party, which has scattered [806] [823] [824] francs across all the palatinates and districts of [493]. So Mr the [577], foreseeing that the sum we were about to pay out to this palatinate would achieve nothing with nobles long since won over by our adversaries, judged it best not to risk it for nothing.

I am, Sir, your very humble and very obedient servant Boeven. [998] [875]

P.S. At Nowogród it proved impossible to get the nobles to include the rehabilitation of Sieur Abramowicz in the deputies' instructions. Most of them, who have lawsuits before the [741], fear that [345] would make them pay dearly for such a step.
