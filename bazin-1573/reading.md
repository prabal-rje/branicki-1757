# Jean Bazin to Henri de Valois, king elect of Poland, September 1573: reading

BnF, Cinq cents de Colbert 338, pp. 191 to 198. French, in cipher, with passages in clear.

Read from the ciphertext alone. I made this text before I knew of the print of 1867, and I did not correct it against the print. `check.md` gives the check and the order of the pages.

**Marks.**

- This reading gives only the stretches where the read is firm. `decryption.txt` gives every line.
- I correct wrong letters from context and write the words in normal spelling of the time. Compare each passage with `decryption.txt`.
- «…» is a word in clear that looks like a code name. It is not read here.
- Words in [brackets] are mine. The key did not give them. `[?]` marks a doubtful word.
- `…` marks a stretch that does not read.
- The numbers match the passages of `translation.md`.
