## In clear (a pass's reading of the scan, not part of the decipherment)

15. (p. 196) The reason is that at the estates held at Warsaw, at the time of the election, the day of your coronation was fixed for the third of October, and in the same way it was agreed that the burial of the said late king would take place eight [days] before. Those of Little Poland have summoned the nobility for Saint Michael's day to attend the said burial, and are having the body brought to Warsaw … The Lithuanians [?] argue that your coronation cannot be on the third of October … [that it is] reasonable that the burial be put off until the week before your coronation, as otherwise it would cause them great trouble and great cost to make two journeys from Lithuania to here … The archbishop and those of Great Poland are of the same opinion, but those of Little Poland cannot be persuaded, and give as their excuse the cost … of keeping those who guard the body …
16. (p. 198) And that, Sire, is the whole state [of things here?]. I will not fail to send further news to Your Majesty as soon as I can learn anything worthy of it. I beg Your Majesty most humbly to forgive me if I have tired you with this long and badly ordered despatch; but the wish to leave nothing out has made me write at greater length … And so, Sire, I pray God to keep Your Majesty in perfect health, most happy … From Cracow, the 20th [?] of September 1573.
